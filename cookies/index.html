Cookie policy

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in the same way. We use the word “cookies” for all of them.

How we use cookies

The ICM website is built to work without tracking. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies or social media tracking, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Cookies on our website

Our own website. The pages of icm.energy do not set cookies of their own and do not store data in your browser to identify you.
Third-party content. The contact form on our Talk to us page is provided by Fillout. When you load the form, Fillout may set cookies or similar technologies needed to show and submit it. Their use is governed by Fillout’s own policies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, parts of the website, such as the contact form, may not work as intended. If you prefer, you can also email us at info@icm.energy instead of using the form.

If this changes

If we start using analytics or other cookies that need consent, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.

More information

See our privacy statement for how we handle personal data, or contact us at info@icm.energy.
