About The Independent Capacity Market

Who we are

The ICM serves those who add capacity to the grid, but are in the dark about their potential.

Greater transparency strengthens the grid. It rewards optimizers that perform, makes batteries easier to finance and gives system operators access to more reliable flexible capacity.

Independence is central to how we work. We do not own, install or optimize batteries, so we have no stake in any outcome other than our clients’ results. We show clients how far their battery is from its full potential, and help them capture the rest.

Our story

How the ICM started

Battery storage is becoming a cornerstone of a resilient power grid. Batteries store surplus solar and wind power, relieve congestion and help keep grid frequency at 50 Hz.

The Netherlands had around 3 GWh installed at the end of 2025 and is projected to reach around 25 GWh by 2030, an eightfold increase.

Yet, for most, battery revenues are a black box, and so is the route to a better optimizer: there is no objective information on which optimizers exist, what their track record is or how they could perform on your site.

The ICM was founded in Amsterdam in March 2026 to close this information gap.

Sources: SolarPower Europe, European Market Outlook for Battery Storage; TaiyangNews, Europe’s battery storage capacity exceeds 100 GWh.

About the role

Batteries are becoming central to a secure power system based on solar and wind. The Netherlands had around 3 GWh of battery storage installed at the end of 2025 and is projected to reach around 25 GWh by 2030, an eightfold increase.

For most owners, battery revenues remain opaque. The ICM benchmarks performance against the full potential of each site and monitors it proactively, so that every battery is used as efficiently as possible.

As our Business Developer, you lead our commercial expansion among owners and installers of >3 MW batteries. We are looking for a sales juggernaut in the making. Someone who builds relationships and grows our network in the Dutch BESS space and creates more value for our batteries under management.

What you bring

  • 3–6 years of experience in B2B consultative sales, ideally in SaaS, energy or financial services
  • A track record of turning first conversations into signed contracts and long-term client relationships
  • A network among battery owners, installers or developers, or the drive to build one quickly
  • Preferred: familiarity with the BESS landscape (meters, EMS, optimizers) and the sales cycle within it
  • Native-level Dutch and strong English

If you meet most, but not all, of these criteria, we encourage you to apply.

What we offer

  • The opportunity to participate in one of the fastest-growing sectors in energy, at the intersection of batteries, grid congestion and power markets
  • Full ownership from your first week, in a small team coached by senior industry leaders. We combine corporate excellence with startup speed.
  • As a starting point, a one-year contract with a salary around the Dutch modal income (EUR 40–50k), plus a performance bonus and equity options
  • Freedom to choose your own approach and to work remotely, with room to co-lead the sales team as we scale
  • A crash course in all things BESS, including how batteries create value: day-ahead, intraday, imbalance, FCR, aFRR, mFRR and redispatch

Full-time or part-time (minimum 3 days a week). Based in Amsterdam, with some travel to visit installers and owners. You report to the founder. Apply with your CV, what motivates you to work for the ICM and what your goals are for the first 3 months.
