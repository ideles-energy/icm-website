Who we are

The ICM serves those who add capacity to the grid, but are in the dark about their potential.

Our story

How the ICM started

Batteries are becoming the backbone of a resilient grid. Europe already has more than 100 GWh of battery storage installed, and the Netherlands is expected to reach around 25 GWh by 2030.

Batteries strengthen the grid. They store surplus solar and wind power, relieve congested grids and help keep the frequency at 50 Hz.

Yet most battery owners are flying blind. They have no overview of the optimizers on the market or how they compare, and no reference for what their own site could deliver.

The ICM was founded in Amsterdam in March 2026 to close this information gap. We serve those who add capacity to the grid, but are in the dark about the potential of their assets.

Sources: SolarPower Europe, European Market Outlook for Battery Storage; TaiyangNews, Europe’s battery storage capacity exceeds 100 GWh.
