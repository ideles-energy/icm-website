Our story

Who we are

The ICM serves those who add capacity to the grid, but are in the dark on the potential of their assets.

We believe in a new standard: an independent benchmark based on real assets, backtesting that looks at all constraints, access to trusted optimizers, and switching made less costly and less risky.

A more transparent flex space rewards optimizers that perform, builds a stronger grid in uncertain times, and delivers more affordable power to us all.

Independent insight for BESS owners in the Netherlands

Owners of battery energy storage systems (BESS) share one question: is my battery earning what it should? Revenues per MW differ widely between comparable batteries, and an optimizer report rarely shows why.

The Independent Capacity Market (the ICM) is an independent energy tech company that helps BESS owners, installers and developers answer that question. We are not an optimizer and we own no batteries. With the BESS Configurator we size and value the right system for your site. With the BESS Backtest we measure how much of your battery's full potential its optimizer captures. With the BESS Broker we request and compare quotes from trusted optimizers and guide the switch when there is more to earn.

The result is a BESS that keeps earning, backed by an independent view you can share with your bank, installer or insurer.
