Batteries at work

Where batteries already make a difference

The Netherlands

Why batteries?

Batteries store electricity when there is plenty and release it when it is scarce. That lets businesses and homes do more with the grid connection they already have, relieves congested grids and helps keep the frequency at 50 Hz as solar and wind power grow.

Click a dot on the map to see a battery that is already making a difference.

Almere

A distribution centre on a 55 kW connection

55 kW → 400 kW

Prologis opened a 23,000 m² distribution centre on a site where the grid operator can deliver only 55 kW until at least 2035. Solar panels, batteries and smart control give the site about 400 kW. A gas generator only steps in when needed.

Source: Het Financieele Dagblad

Cromvoirt

125 holiday homes on 41 kW

125 homes · 41 kW

At holiday park Landgoed De Zandleij, 125 holiday homes share a grid connection of just 41 kW. The park charges its batteries at night, when the grid has room, and uses that power during the day.

Source: Het Financieele Dagblad
