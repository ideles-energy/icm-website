BESS Calculator

What could a battery have earned on the Dutch wholesale markets from January to September 2026? The calculator answers that for one specific set-up: MW/MWh, grid connection and dispatch constraints included.

  • Real context, not a virtual battery. Results follow the limits of the actual site, such as import and export caps and contract terms.
  • Calibrated with realised revenues. Each comparison with actual settlements from owners, installers and analysts sharpens the model.
  • Open about its limits. It covers day-ahead and imbalance only, as intraday data is not publicly available.
Open to contributions. Realised revenues shared by owners, installers and analysts are used to calibrate the model. Please email ideles@icm.energy to contribute.

NL BESS Revenue Report

A periodic, anonymised view of what batteries in the Netherlands earn per MW, based on aggregated data. No asset, owner or optimizer is ever named.

  • Aggregated and anonymised. Results are only shown for groups of assets, never for a single battery.
  • Like for like. Revenues are compared per MW and per duration, so a 1-hour and a 2-hour battery are not lumped together.
  • In your inbox. Free, with each new edition.
