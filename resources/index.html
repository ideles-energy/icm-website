The Netherlands

The role of battery storage

Battery energy storage systems (BESS) are becoming a cornerstone of a resilient power grid. They store surplus solar and wind power, relieve congestion and help keep grid frequency at 50 Hz.

Dutch businesses are increasingly deploying batteries to make better use of constrained grid connections. SolarPower Europe projects that battery storage in the Netherlands will reach around 25 GWh by 2030.

Almere, Flevoland

A distribution centre using ~400 kW on a 55 kW connection

Logistics developer Prologis opened a 23,000 m² distribution centre in Almere on a site where the grid operator can only provide 55 kW until at least 2035. Thanks to solar panels, battery storage and smart energy management, the power available on site sits at ~400 kW. A gas generator operates only when additional supply is required.

Source: Het Financieele Dagblad

Cromvoirt, Noord-Brabant

125 holiday homes on a ~41 kW connection

Holiday park Landgoed De Zandleij runs 125 holiday homes on a ~41 kW grid connection. Thanks to batteries that charge overnight, when local electricity demand is low, the holiday homes have sufficient power during the day.

Source: Het Financieele Dagblad
