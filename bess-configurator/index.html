What you get

BESS Configurator in short

  • Size: how many MW of power and MWh of storage fit your site, your load and your PV or wind profile.
  • Business case: payback period, net present value and return on investment of the project.
  • Revenue streams: energy arbitrage, peak shaving, and the imbalance and ancillary service markets.
  • Lifetime: degradation from cycling and ageing, so you know when capacity declines over 10 to 15 years.
How it works

Simple steps, from first call to result

Share your context

Your site, grid connection and load or generation profile, and what you want from the battery.

We size and simulate

We test system sizes against your profile and the revenue streams that apply to your site.

You see the business case

A clear comparison of options, with the assumptions written down, ready for your bank, partner or board.

Who it is for. Owners and developers planning a battery next to solar, wind or a large energy user, and installers who size systems for customers.

Let's talk about your project

Start with a 45-minute call about your context and objectives (for example a portfolio of 3-30 MW BESS assets, or trading revenues below expectations). Afterwards you receive a full proposal.
