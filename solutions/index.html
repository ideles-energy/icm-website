Frequently asked questions

Why do battery owners use the ICM?

To know what their battery could earn, and whether it earns that. We compare your revenue with the full potential of your site, and when there is more to earn we help you find a better optimizer. Because we own no batteries and are not an optimizer, you can share our numbers with your bank, installer and insurer.

Is the ICM for my battery?

The ICM serves owners and developers of batteries of roughly 3 to 30 MW, and the installers who work with them. You do not need to be an expert in power markets.

I don't know if my optimizer is any good. Can the ICM tell me?

Yes. The BESS Backtest compares what your battery earned with the most it could have earned on your site. The result is a capture rate: the share of the full potential that was realised, and what holds it back.

What if my battery earns less than it could?

You decide what happens next. If the result objectively falls short, the BESS Broker can help you compare optimizers, test what they would have earned and guide the switch.

Is the ICM really independent?

Yes. We do not own BESS assets. We are not an optimizer. Nor will we ever be.

What do I need to get approved?

Your work email and a few project details. After approval we ask for read access to your optimizer, or three months of settlements, plus an authorisation to read your meter data from your metering company.

When does it make sense to start with the ICM?

Before you build, to size the right system with the BESS Configurator. After your battery is running, to see whether it earns what it should with the BESS Backtest. And whenever the numbers say there is more to earn, to find a better optimizer with the BESS Broker.

How do installers work with the ICM?

Installers can size a system with the BESS Configurator before the sale, include a BESS Backtest at delivery so every customer gets an independent view of what the battery earns, and refer owners who want a better optimizer to the BESS Broker.
