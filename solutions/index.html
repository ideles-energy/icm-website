Frequently Asked Questions
What is a BESS Backtest?

The BESS Backtest analyses the historical usage data of your battery and reveals unused potential. The result is called the “Capture Rate”: the percentage of the full potential that was realised. It shows whether a battery earns what it should, and why it might be held back.

What makes the ICM independent?

We don’t own batteries and we are not an optimizer. That means we don’t compete on the energy markets and we don’t care which optimizer you use. We simply provide you with accurate numbers you can share with your lender, insurer, installer and optimizer.

Who orders a BESS Backtest, the battery owner or the installer?

Both are possible: installers can offer a BESS Backtest at or even before delivery, so every customer gets an independent view of what the battery can earn. Owners can also order one directly. Talk to us about how this works for you.

What if my battery isn’t earning to its full potential?

You decide what happens next. If the result objectively falls short, the first step is contacting the optimizer. We’ll help you review or, when desired, aid you in requesting and comparing quotes from additional optimizers, test what they would have earned, and guide the switch.

Why work with the ICM?

We are an independent entity serving both your profit margin and helping solidify the national electricity network. No other incentives, transparent communication, all while delivering results.

Can I trust the ICM’s numbers?

Mathematics, econometrics, and complex data analysis tools. Programmed by us, simple to use for you. Our reason for existing is the accuracy of our data and we stand behind it with a confident stance. Markets are always in flux and that’s what makes the challenge worthwhile. We are constantly adjusting our calculations, so you don’t have to. Used information resources are always listed for transparency.

What data does the ICM need, and how is it handled?

The more data we have, the more accurate our report. Optimizer reports, meter data per 15 minutes, data from the energy management system for downtime, and the documents on your grid connection and optimizer mandate. We guide you through the list, and your installer or metering service provider can often deliver most of it.

What can the ICM do for my installer?

Installers can use the ICM in three ways:

  • Size a system with the BESS Calculator in the pre-construction phase
  • Include a BESS Backtest at delivery so every customer gets an independent view of what the battery earns
  • Refer owners who want an efficient optimizer to our BESS Broker

Talk to us to see what fits your way of working.

Why do installers offer the BESS Backtest to their customers?

A useful tool is the best gift, it builds trust. Installers can offer a free BESS Backtest at delivery, so every customer gets an independent view of what the battery earns. It shows you stand behind your product.

Am I increasing my profits or improving the grid?

Both, that’s the beauty! A battery that earns the most it can is obviously good for your bottom line, but also makes your battery usage respond to what the energy market needs. Profits therefore directly transfer into a more stable grid.
