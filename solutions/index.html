Frequently asked questions

What does the ICM do?

We help BESS owners get the most out of their battery: size the right system with the BESS Configurator, monitor performance with the BESS Backtest, and find a better optimizer with the BESS Broker when there is more to earn.

Who is it for?

Owners and developers of batteries of roughly 3 to 30 MW, and the installers who work with them. You do not need to be an expert in power markets.

Is the ICM independent?

Yes. We do not own BESS assets. We are not an optimizer. Nor will we ever be. That is why our numbers can be shared with your bank, installer and insurer.

What do I need to get started?

Your work email and a few project details. After approval we ask for read access to your optimizer, or three months of settlements, plus an authorisation to read your meter data from your metering company.

What does the BESS Backtest measure?

It compares what your battery earned with the most it could have earned on your site. The result is a capture rate: the share of the full potential that was realised, and what holds it back.

What if my battery earns less than expected?

You decide what happens next. If the result objectively falls short, the BESS Broker can help you compare optimizers, test what they would have earned and guide the switch.

How do installers work with the ICM?

Installers can size a system with the BESS Configurator before the sale, include a BESS Backtest at delivery, and refer owners who want a better optimizer to the BESS Broker.
