Where it leaks

  • Suboptimal configuration. The MW/MWh installed directly affects your return on investment. A poor set-up can cost you from day one.
  • Underperforming optimizer. New players with better algorithms keep entering the market. Staying with yours can pose a risk.
  • Too little monitoring. Without knowing how far you are from your full potential, your BESS revenue is a black box. Underperformance can go unnoticed for years.
