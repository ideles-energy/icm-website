The BESS Benchmark compares what your battery actually earned per MW with comparable batteries in the Netherlands. Real revenues of real assets, anonymised, independent of any optimizer.

Why it matters

Independent information on what a battery earns is missing

Two batteries in exactly the same set-up can differ by tens of thousands of euros per MW per year. Without a benchmark, an owner cannot tell whether that is the market, the set-up or the optimizer.

The rules

Simple rules, the same for every member

Membership

Share at least 6 months of history and stay a member for at least 3 months. After that, the BESS Benchmark can be cancelled monthly.

Symbolic contribution

Members pay 0.5% of what their battery earned that month. At €15,000 per MW, that is €75 per MW. Historical months are free. Refer 5 members and your contribution drops to 0.1% for 6 months.

Give to get

You see the benchmark only for the months you contributed to. The same principle applies to installers, advisers and financiers who want access.

No news, good news

We contact you only if a project earned less than 80% of the benchmark in at least 6 of the last 12 months. Otherwise, you check the benchmark whenever you like.

Always anonymous

A benchmark is shown only for groups of at least 5 assets from at least 3 organisations. We never publish results per optimizer.

Independent

The ICM owns no batteries and is not an optimizer. We use outcome data only – never bids, prices or trading strategies.

Real data, not a model

Why real revenues beat a simulated index

A simulated revenue index

  • Models a theoretical battery with fixed assumptions
  • Assumes a full grid connection and no downtime
  • Cannot tell you how your optimizer performs

The BESS Benchmark

  • Realised revenues from optimizers' credit invoices
  • Captures time-bound transport rights (TDTR, TBTR), congestion contracts (CSC, CBC), downtime and degradation
  • Compares your battery with batteries set up like yours
Questions

Frequently asked

What data do I need to share?

Per battery and per month: gross revenue as stated on your optimizer's credit invoice, the optimizer fee and any hours the battery was unavailable. At the start, at least the six most recent months. We do not ask for bids, prices or trading strategies.

Does my optimizer contract allow me to share this?

Often it does. If your contract restricts sharing, you and your optimizer sign a short mandate so the optimizer can send the figures to us directly.

Who sees my data?

Only you see your own figures. Other members only see anonymised benchmarks of at least 5 assets from at least 3 organisations. We never show results per optimizer or rank optimizers.

Which batteries can join?

Battery storage in the Netherlands traded under a merchant contract. Our focus is assets of 3 to 30 MW; we decide on each project in the intake.

What does it cost?

A symbolic contribution of 0.5% of the gross revenue of each month you are a member, excluding VAT. Historical months and months with negative revenue are free.

Can I share my results with my financier?

Yes. Your results can be printed as a report with your own figures and the benchmark – never data of other organisations.

How is the benchmark calculated?

Total revenue of all peer assets divided by their capacity and active hours, expressed in EUR per MW per year. Every rule is in the methodology.
