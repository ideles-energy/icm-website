Privacy statement

About this statement

This privacy statement explains how Independent Capacity Market B.V. (“the ICM”, “we”, “us”), Amsterdam, handles personal data collected through icm.energy and through our contact with owners, installers, optimizers and other visitors. It applies to this website. Other ICM services, such as the BESS Benchmark app or a BESS Backtest, may come with additional terms or notices.

Who is responsible

The ICM is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@icm.energy.

Personal data we collect

We collect the data you give us when you use the contact form or email us: your name, company, email address, the size of your portfolio or project, and your message. We also receive technical data that every website receives, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser requests a page. We do not ask for special categories of personal data and we ask you not to send them to us.

How we use personal data

We use your data to answer your request, plan a 45-minute call and prepare a proposal, to keep our website secure and working, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Legal bases

We rely on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and keeping our website secure, on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, on a legal obligation where one applies, and on your consent where we ask for it.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run our business, under agreements that protect your data. Fillout receives the contact form on our behalf. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, serves the website. We may also share data with advisers or authorities where the law requires.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where that is the case we rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep contact details for as long as needed to follow up on your request and at most two years after our last contact, unless you become a client or member, or the law requires us to keep them longer.

How we protect it

We limit access to personal data to people who need it, use trusted providers and keep our systems up to date. No online service is completely secure, so please do not send us confidential data you do not need to share.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent you have given. Email info@icm.energy and we will respond within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Cookies

The ICM website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. See our cookie policy for details.

Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The date below shows when it was last changed.
